About

I'm a freelance journalist with five years of experience writing human-centric cultural deep dives across technology, finance, and cryptocurrency. I'm known for my original reporting and nose for wacky stories, which has landed my byline in VICE, The Verge, and Fortune.

For two years, I was a staff reporter at Decrypt, which sharpened my tools in establishing a beat, landing exclusive scoops, and building a wide network of sources. In this role, I established myself as the publication's go-to writer for all things cultural, weird, or wacky — and produced some of my best work.

After taking a career break to travel across South America, I am now looking for freelance work. While much of my experience lies within the financial sector, I tend to approach topics with a cultural angle I adopted early in my freelance career as a music journalist.
